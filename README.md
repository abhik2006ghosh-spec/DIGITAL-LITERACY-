# DIGITAL-LITERACY-
Task Summary
Task 1 – Digital Literacy Awareness Infographic

In this task, I created an infographic to explain the concept of digital literacy and its importance for students. I used an online design tool to visually present key topics such as safe internet practices, useful digital tools for students, and maintaining a professional online presence. The infographic helped simplify important information so that it can be easily understood by my peers.

Task 2 – Student Digital Portfolio

For this task, I created and updated my professional profiles on platforms such as GitHub and LinkedIn. I added my educational information and created a GitHub profile README describing my academic background and learning goals. This task helped me understand the importance of maintaining a professional digital presence that can be used for networking, internships, and future career opportunities.

Task 3 – Coding & Collaboration Platforms

In this task, I explored online platforms used for coding practice and academic collaboration. I created an account on a coding practice platform and completed a beginner-level programming challenge. I also created a Google Form quiz about digital literacy awareness for students. This helped me learn how digital tools can support learning, assessment, and collaboration.

Task 4 – Email Etiquette & Social Media Responsibility

This task focused on professional digital communication. I wrote two professional emails—one requesting an assignment extension from a professor and another expressing interest in a summer internship. I also created a checklist of social media do’s and don’ts to encourage responsible online behavior among students.

Task 5 – Cybercrime Awareness

In this task, I researched cybercrime and wrote a case study explaining how online fraud can occur and who is usually targeted. I also created a “Stay Safe Online” checklist with practical safety tips, including precautions for UPI and online payments. This task increased my awareness of cyber threats and the importance of practicing safe online habits.
